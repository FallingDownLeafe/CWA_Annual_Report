## 4

<!-- 第 4 條：RL 引測至潮位站水準點（TGBM），TGBM 高程依內政部一等水準網
             以基隆港平均海水面為零點（TWVD2001）定義；
             離島（澎湖、金門等）使用當地平均海水面（LMSL）。 -->
<!-- 中文文字貼於此行下方 -->
基準面關係圖：各潮位站旁邊設有潮位站水準點(TGBM, tide gauge benchmark)，其設置是為與內政部公告之一等水準網聯測之用，亦即利用精密水準測量決定潮位站水準點與附近一等水準點(first order benchmark)間之正高差，進而利用一等水準點的高程，推算各潮位站水準點的高程。如此潮位紀錄可推算到相對於臺灣高程基準(TWVD2001)之高度。

<!-- 英文文字貼於此行下方 -->
The instrument reference level of each tide station is calibrated to a tide gauge benchmark beside the station. The elevation at a tide gauge benchmark is based on the first order leveling network of the Ministry of Interior leveling survey, on which the mean sea level of Keelung Port is treated as the zero datum level for the Taiwan area (TaiWan Vertical Datum 2001, TWVD2001). Therefore, the tide records of each tide station should be interpreted as the height above TWVD2001 , excluding the outlying islands.
Note that the tide records of station in outlying islands such as Penghu and Kinmen are referred to Local Mean Sea Level (LMSL).

## 9

<!-- 第 9 條：逐月統計年報定義 -->
逐月潮位統計年報表：
Annual Summary of Tidal Statistics

### 9-1 平均潮位 Mean Sea Level (MSL)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
平均潮位：年平均值係累計月平均值 1 年之總平均。
Mean Sea Level: Monthly/Annual MSL is the mean of the total hourly tidal values of that month/year.


### 9-2 高低潮次數 Number of High / Low Tides

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
高低潮次數：每月高低潮的次數總和。
The amount of High/Low tides: The amount of high/low tides in that month/year.


### 9-3 平均高潮位／平均低潮位 (MHWL / MLWL)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
平均高(低)潮位：係由當年各月之每日較高(低)的高(低)潮的平均值。
Mean High/Low Water Level: Monthly/Annual MHWL/MLWL is the mean of higher high/lower low tides of that month/year.


### 9-4 最高天文潮／最低天文潮 (HAT / LAT)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最高(低)天文潮：當年各月天文潮之最高(低)潮位。
Highest/Lowest Astronomical Tide level: Monthly/Annual HAT/LAT is the highest/lowest astronomical tides of that month/year.


### 9-5 平均潮差 (MR)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
平均潮差：當年各月平均高潮位與平均低潮位之差值。
Mean Tidal Range: Monthly/Annual MR is the difference between MHWL and MLWL of that month/year.



### 9-6 最高高潮位／最低低潮位 (HHWL / LLWL)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最高高(低低)潮位：當年各月觀測值中之最高高(低低)潮位。
Highest High Water Level or Lowest Low Water Level: Monthly/Annual HHWL/LLWL is the highest/lowest among high/low tides of that month/year.


### 9-7 最大天文潮差 Max Astronomical Tide Range (MATR / STR)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最大天文潮差：當年各月最高天文潮位與最低天文潮位之差值。
Maximum Tide Range of Astronomical Tide: Monthly/Annual STR is the difference between HAT and LAT of that month/year.


### 9-8 最大潮差 (MTR)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最大潮差：當年最高高潮位與最低低潮位之差值。
Maximum Tidal Range: Monthly/Annual MTR is the difference between HHWL and LLWL of that year.

## 10

<!-- 第 10 條：逐日高低潮月報定義（各子項自行換行，標題留空不填也可） -->
逐日潮位統計月報表：
Monthly Summary of Tidal Statistics

### 10-1 高潮／低潮 High Tide / Low Tide

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
高(低)潮位：每日所發生之海面規律升降現象，當海面上升至相對最高時稱為高潮或滿潮；當海面下降至相對最低時稱為低潮或乾潮。本表依序記載每日所發生之第 1 次高(低)潮及第 2 次高(低)潮之潮時及潮高。
High/low tides: The time that the water level rises/falls to the extreme point is different every day. The water level rises to the highest point, called the high tide, and falls to the lowest point, called the low tide. The first and the second high/low tides of each day are recorded in their order.


### 10-2 平均海面 Mean Sea Level (MSL)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
平均潮位：月平均值係累計逐時潮位值 1 個月之總平均。
Mean Sea Level: Monthly MSL is the mean of the total hourly tidal values of that month.


### 10-3 平均高潮位／平均低潮位 Mean High / Low Water Level (MHWL / MLWL)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
平均高(低)潮位：係取每日之較高(低)的高(低)潮的平均值。
Mean High/Low Water Level: Monthly MHWL/MLWL is the mean of higher high/lower low tides of that month.


### 10-4 平均潮差 Mean Tidal Range (MR)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
平均潮差：當月平均高潮位與平均低潮位之差值。
Mean Tidal Range: Monthly MR is the difference between MHWL and MLWL of that month.


### 10-5 最大潮差 Maximum Tidal Range (MTR)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最大潮差：當月最高高潮位與最低低潮位之差值。
Maximum Tidal Range: Monthly MTR is the difference between HHWL and LLWL of that month.


### 10-6 最高高潮位／最低低潮位 Highest High / Lowest Low Water Level (HHWL / LLWL)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最高(低)潮位：當月觀測值中之最高(低)潮位。
Highest High Water Level or Lowest Low Water Level: Monthly HHWL/LLWL is the highest/lowest among high/low tides of that month.


### 10-7 最高天文潮／最低天文潮 Highest / Lowest Astronomical Tide (HAT / LAT)

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
最高(低)天文潮位：當月天文潮之最高(低)潮位。
Highest Astronomical Tide level or Lowest Astronomical Tide level：Monthly HAT/LAT is the highest/lowest astronomical tides of that month.


### 10-8 暴潮偏差 Surge

<!-- 文字貼於此行下方 -->
暴潮偏差：當月最高潮位與最高天文潮位之差值。
Surge: Monthly Surge is the difference between HHWL and HAT of that month.
Storm surge can be defined as the abnormal rise in water level caused by wind and
pressure forces of hurricane.
